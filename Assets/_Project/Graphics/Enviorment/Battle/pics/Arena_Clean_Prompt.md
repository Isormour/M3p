# Oczyszczenie aren

Narzędzie: wbudowany imagegen. Ten sam prompt zastosowany osobno do czterech oryginalnych aren. Wyniki zapisane z przyrostkiem _Clean.png. Edycja generatywna redukuje szum i upraszcza drobne szczegóły powierzchni.

## Prompt

Edit the provided image only to remove the distracting fine texture noise. Input image is the exact edit target, not loose inspiration. Apply a strong clean stylized material denoising pass: eliminate fine mottled speckles, tiny random chips and pockmarks, stippled microtexture, grain and crunchy noisy highlights on the stone surfaces. Replace noisy microtexture with smooth coherent broad stone planes and clean soft tonal gradients, like a polished high quality stylized 3D game render. Keep major stone blocks, large cracks, bevels, silhouettes and geometric edges sharp. Preserve the scene, all object placement, framing, camera, arena platform shape, architecture, atmosphere, original palette, overall lighting and original aspect ratio exactly. Keep meaningful moss/ice/lava details as clean larger shapes rather than speckle. Do not blur the entire image, do not add detail, objects, text or sharpening grain. The result should look noticeably cleaner and less busy, especially the foreground stone faces, while remaining the same arena.

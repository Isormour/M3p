## Shared specification

Polished stylized 3D game-character modeling reference matching the supplied project images: chunky chibi fantasy proportions, crisp sculpted forms, hand-painted PBR feel, neutral studio background, soft grounding shadow. One centered full-body character in a symmetrical front T-pose, arms horizontal, palms forward, complete crown and feet visible. Clean continuous anatomy suitable for later 3D modeling. No weapon, base, scenery, text, logo, or watermark. Avoid photorealism, painterly 2D art, thin realistic proportions, extra limbs, cropping, and busy backgrounds.

## V2 — tree-form correction

Each first-pass image was used as its own edit target and palette/material reference. The anatomy was redesigned to read first as an ancient walking tree rather than a humanoid forest person:

- one continuous, irregular trunk instead of a human torso, waist, and hips
- crooked lateral boughs instead of shoulders and muscular arms
- natural twig forks instead of palms and fingers
- split trunk supports dissolving into tangled roots instead of legs and feet
- eyes and a mouth suggested only by glowing hollows, knots, and bark cracks
- no separate head, human facial anatomy, beard, elf ears, clothing, belt, medallion, armor, bracers, or knee guards

The original species, seasonal foliage, glow color, polished stylized 3D rendering, neutral studio presentation, and full modeling-reference silhouette were preserved for every variant.
